Our Story

From small farms to your kitchen

real100agro began with a simple promise: ghee the way our grandparents made it — grass-fed desi cows, slow bilona churning, and nothing added after.

Where it started

Why families trust real100agro

We work with small farms, pay fairly, and keep every batch traceable — from the cow you can name to the jar on your shelf. No industrial shortcuts, no anonymous milk pools.

  • Grass-fed desi cows, no hormones
  • Traditional bilona, never machine-pressed
  • Lab tested for purity in every batch

The bilona way

Slow churning, golden grain

Curd is hand-churned into butter, then gently clarified until the ghee turns golden and grainy. That patience is the difference you taste in every spoon.

Each small batch is packed fresh so the aroma stays rich — the way it should when it leaves the farm kitchen.
