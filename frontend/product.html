Description

Our A2 desi cow bilona ghee is made in small batches from curd churned by hand, then slow-cooked until the milk solids separate and the ghee turns clear gold. The result is a grainy texture and a warm, nutty aroma that holds up for cooking, tadka, and everyday meals.

How to use

Use a spoonful for tadka, drizzle over dal and rice, or take a teaspoon warm in the morning. Store in a cool, dry place with the lid closed — no refrigeration needed.
