From farm to jar

The traditional journey of our ghee

Five careful steps. No machines pressing the cream — only bilona, heat, and time.

  1. 01

    Milking

    Fresh milk from grass-fed desi cows, gathered by hand each morning.

  2. 02

    Preparing curd

    Milk sets overnight into thick curd — the base of true bilona ghee.

  3. 03

    Churning

    Hand-churned with a wooden bilona until butter rises to the top.

  4. 04

    Heating

    Slow-cooked until the butter clarifies into golden, grainy ghee.

  5. 05

    Packaging

    Poured warm into jars and sealed — batch by batch, never rushed.
